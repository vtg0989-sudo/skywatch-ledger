How to read these files

Unresolved isn't alien. Many files carry the label "unresolved." That means the government couldn't identify the object with the data it had. It doesn't mean it found anything non-human, and none of the releases so far contains proof of non-human technology.

A lot of it is old or already public. Several batches mix brand-new records with Project Blue Book–era files and papers that were released before in other forms. The newer military sensor videos are where most of the fresh material is.

Redactions vary. Some batches are mostly clean. Release 06 was partly redacted in 89% of its files.

Counts can shift. The Pentagon has re-issued at least one release with added or replaced files, and independent trackers count multi-part files differently. The numbers here follow the per-file counts kept by UAP Browser and WARUFO. For the definitive list, check the official archive.
